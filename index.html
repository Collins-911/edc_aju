Who We Are

The AJU Enterprise Development Center (EDC) equips students with real-world vocational and entrepreneurial skills. We bridge the gap between education and enterprise through hands-on training programs.

What We Do

Our core training programs and enterprise activities

Agriculture

🐔 Poultry Farming

We train students in modern poultry management — from brooding and feeding to record-keeping and marketing of poultry products.

Agriculture

🌾 Crop Farming

Students engage in practical crop production including planting, irrigation, and harvesting to build sustainable agricultural enterprises.

Vocational

🧵 Sewing & Fashion

Our tailoring and fashion design program teaches cutting, stitching, and garment production — preparing students for the textile industry.

Agriculture

🐟 Fish Farming

Students learn aquaculture techniques including pond management, fish feeding, and processing to run profitable fish farming businesses.

Vocational

🍞 Bakery & Food Production

We offer training in bread, pastry, and confectionery production equipping students with skills to start food businesses.

Technology

💻 ICT & Digital Skills

Basic computer literacy, graphic design, and digital entrepreneurship training for students entering today's digital economy.
